Who this fits

A growing team where the repeating work isn't one chain — it's several. Front desk, scheduling, follow-ups, and paperwork all compete for the same people's time. Studio fits when you want one partner to organize the whole front office, in a sensible order, instead of patching it one task at a time.

The benefits, in plain terms

What this package is actually for — the problems it takes off your plate.

The whole front office, one plan

Several workflows managed together, under one set of boundaries and one monthly arrangement — not a pile of separate tools and logins to babysit.

Work in the right order

Workflows are prioritized by what costs you the most time or lost business first. You don't pay to automate the trivial while the expensive problem waits.

Handoffs between workflows, handled

A booking that creates a follow-up, an inquiry that becomes paperwork — when the workflows touch each other, those connections are part of the design.

A consistent experience for customers

The same promptness and tone whether a customer is asking, booking, or being followed up with — your business feels organized from the outside.

Your team gets its time back for real work

The repeating layer is covered across departments, so your people spend their hours on customers and judgment calls, not re-typing and chasing.

One accountable place

Questions, changes, and reviews for every workflow go to one managed service — with the full map in writing, so nothing depends on one person's head.

What's included

Everything below is part of this package. The exact workflow is agreed with you in writing before setup starts.

  • Everything in Operations
  • Mapping of several workflows across your business, in writing
  • A priority order agreed with you: highest-value workflow first
  • Approval points documented per workflow, in one place
  • Connections between workflows scoped where they touch (for example: a booking that triggers a follow-up)
  • Review passes over sample cases for each workflow before it goes live
  • One consolidated written handoff: every workflow, every boundary, every next step

What it looks like in real life

One example, start to finish.

Example: a week with Studio running

  1. New inquiries are answered and organized the same day they arrive, with approval items flagged to you.
  2. Bookings confirm themselves and send reminders; follow-ups go out on schedule without anyone remembering.
  3. Routine intake paperwork is collected and prepared for your review instead of scattered across inboxes.
  4. You start the week looking at exceptions and approvals — the parts that genuinely need you — not at a pile of repeating chores.

What this package does not include

So there are no surprises later.

  • Unlimited workflows — the set is agreed and written into your scope
  • Custom software development or a full systems rebuild
  • Guaranteed customers, revenue, or response rates
  • Any workflow sending prices, refunds, or commitments without your documented approval

No package guarantees customers or sales. The first step is always a conversation about whether the scope makes sense for your business — asking costs nothing.
