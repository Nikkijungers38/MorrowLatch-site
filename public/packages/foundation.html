Who this fits

A solo owner or small team with one clear pain point — the same questions, the same intake, the same follow-up — who wants to start small, see it work, and stay in control. If one weekly task eats your hours, this is where you begin.

The benefits, in plain terms

What this package is actually for — the problems it takes off your plate.

The same questions stop eating your day

Your agent gives the first response to the questions you answer over and over — hours, services, where you're located, how booking works — using information you approved, the same way every time.

No inquiry sits unanswered

Every new inquiry gets a prompt first touch, even when you're with a customer, on a job, or closed for the day. You come back to an organized request instead of a cold lead.

You stay in control

Anything you name — prices, discounts, refunds, commitments — stops and waits for your approval. That list is written into your plan before setup, not improvised later.

One clear starting point

Instead of 'automate everything,' you get one workflow mapped end to end. Small, defined, and easy to judge: you'll know quickly whether it's helping.

Everything in writing

You receive a handoff covering what the agent does, what it never does, and the approval points — so the arrangement doesn't live in anyone's memory.

A low-risk way to test AI

One workflow, one monthly price, no big build-out. If it earns its keep, Operations is the natural next step; if it doesn't fit, you've learned that cheaply.

What's included

Everything below is part of this package. The exact workflow is agreed with you in writing before setup starts.

  • A scoping conversation to pick the one workflow worth starting with
  • A written map of that workflow as it happens in your business today
  • Your approval points, documented before setup
  • Managed agent setup for that one workflow
  • A review pass over sample cases together before it goes live
  • A written handoff: the agreed work, boundaries, and next steps
  • Inquiries from the work routed to you in your dashboard and by email

What it looks like in real life

One example, start to finish.

Example: the front-desk workflow

  1. A customer asks a common question — hours, services, how to book — and the agent answers from your approved information.
  2. The agent collects what you need: name, contact, and what they're asking for.
  3. If the customer asks about price or anything on your approval list, the agent doesn't guess — it flags it for you.
  4. You see the organized request and pick it up when it actually needs a person.

What this package does not include

So there are no surprises later.

  • More than one workflow (that's Operations and Studio territory)
  • Custom software development or rebuilding your website
  • Guaranteed customers, bookings, or sales
  • Sending prices, discounts, or refunds without your approval — ever

No package guarantees customers or sales. The first step is always a conversation about whether the scope makes sense for your business — asking costs nothing.
