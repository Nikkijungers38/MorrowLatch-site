What Foundation actually means

No jargon — what you're really buying with this package.

Foundation means this: you pick one job that repeats in your business — the same customer questions, the same intake, the same first follow-up — and we set up a managed AI agent to handle that one job, and only that job. It works from information you approve, it stops at the lines you draw, and everything it does is written down. It's the smallest, clearest way to start: one workflow, one monthly price, and proof it helps before you spend another dollar.

Questions people ask about Foundation

The things owners usually want to know before this package makes sense.

What exactly will the agent do all day?

The one workflow we scoped together — for example, answering your most common customer questions, collecting the details you need from each new inquiry, and flagging anything about price for you. It doesn't wander into other jobs; if it isn't in the written scope, it doesn't happen.

Does it talk to my customers directly?

Within the workflow and channels agreed in your scope, yes — using your approved information and wording. Anything unusual, sensitive, or on your approval list is routed to you instead of answered.

What if my one workflow turns out to be bigger than we thought?

Then we say so honestly during scoping. If the job really has extra steps attached — like booking and reminders after the inquiry — that's a sign Operations fits better, and we'll tell you why before you pay for either.

Can I move up later?

Yes. Foundation is designed as the starting step. If the first workflow earns its keep, Operations adds the handoffs around it, and everything already documented carries over.
