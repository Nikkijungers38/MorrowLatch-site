How it works

Three steps, in the open, before anything is automated.

1 — Identify a useful task

We map a repeatable business task as it happens today and decide whether an agent could help.

2 — Document human review

Together we mark the points where a person must check, approve, or stop the work before it continues.

3 — Hand off the agreed next steps

You receive a written handoff covering the agreed work, tool-connection details, approval points, setup notes, and what happens next.
